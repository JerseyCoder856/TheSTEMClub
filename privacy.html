Privacy

Privacy information

The public website collects information only when you intentionally submit a form or use an external payment service. Member account information is stored in the configured Supabase project and is protected by authentication and database access policies.

Contact and volunteer inquiries

Website inquiry forms open your email application so you can review the message before sending it. The website does not claim that a message was delivered when it was not.

Payments and external services

Donation and checkout actions may continue to PayPal. Social media, maps, images, and other external resources are governed by their providers’ privacy practices.
