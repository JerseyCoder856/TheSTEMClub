About The STEM Club

A place to learn,
create, and connect.

Bringing STEM learning and community service together for young adults.

The STEM Club serves people ages 18–27 in underserved communities across South Jersey and the Philadelphia area. Our programs explore electronics, coding, 3D design, environmental topics, community service, and robotics.

Whether you’re exploring a new interest or looking for ways to contribute, connect with us to learn about participation and current opportunities.
